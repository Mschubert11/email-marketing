More Ways to Join the Coop

Whether you're stopping in for chicken, grabbing food after class, or ordering online, there's always a reason to stay connected. Here's how The Coop will build its email community.

CUSTOMER GROUP 01

In-Store Customers

Where & How We Collect Emails

Place QR codes at the register, on tables, and on takeout packaging. Employees can encourage customers to scan and subscribe while ordering. QR codes lead directly to the email sign-up form.

Immediate Value Exchange

Customers receive a 10% off digital coupon for their next order after confirming their subscription.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers receive exclusive discounts, new menu announcements, and early access to limited-time promotions to encourage repeat visits.

CUSTOMER GROUP 02

Iowa State Students

Where & How We Collect Emails

Reach students through Instagram stories, organic social media posts, targeted campus advertisements, and QR code flyers near Iowa State. Promote convenient meals for busy college students.

Immediate Value Exchange

Students receive a digital student discount coupon after signing up and verifying their email address.

Long-Term Value Promise

Students stay subscribed for student-exclusive promotions, late-night meal deals, and special offers during campus events and game days.

CUSTOMER GROUP 03

Online Shoppers

Where & How We Collect Emails

Promote the sign-up form through The Coop's website, online ordering pages, Instagram posts, and digital advertisements. Customers can voluntarily subscribe without being automatically added through a purchase.

Immediate Value Exchange

Customers receive 10% off their next online order after confirming their email subscription.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers receive personalized offers based on their preferences, new menu announcements, and online-ordering promotions to encourage future purchases.

All three customer groups must voluntarily consent to receiving promotional emails. Double opt-in confirmation verifies each subscriber's email address. Every commercial email includes a clear unsubscribe option. Discounts and student offers are proposed campaign incentives.
