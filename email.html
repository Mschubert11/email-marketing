More Ways to Join the Coop

Whether you're grabbing chicken after class, stopping in for your favorite meal, or ordering online, there's always a reason to stay connected. Here's how The Coop plans to build its email community.

CUSTOMER GROUP 01

In-Store Customers

Where & How We Collect Emails

Place QR codes near the register, on tables, and on takeout packaging. Employees can also invite customers to sign up while ordering. The QR code takes customers directly to The Coop's email sign-up form.

Immediate Value Exchange

Customers receive a 10% off coupon for their next order after confirming their email subscription.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers receive exclusive discounts, special menu announcements, and early access to limited-time promotions.

CUSTOMER GROUP 02

Iowa State Students

Where & How We Collect Emails

Promote the email list through Instagram stories, campus flyers with QR codes, and student-focused social media ads. Messaging will highlight affordable meals and deals for busy college students.

Immediate Value Exchange

Students receive access to a special student discount or a digital coupon after signing up and confirming their email address.

Long-Term Value Promise

Students stay subscribed for future student-only deals, late-night meal promotions, and exclusive offers during busy campus events.

CUSTOMER GROUP 03

Online Shoppers

Where & How We Collect Emails

Add an email sign-up opportunity to The Coop's online ordering website. Promote the form through Instagram posts, digital advertisements, and links on social media profiles.

Immediate Value Exchange

New subscribers receive 10% off their next online order after confirming their subscription.

Long-Term Value Promise

Subscribers receive personalized promotions, updates on new menu items, and exclusive online ordering deals that encourage repeat purchases.

The Coop will only collect emails from customers who voluntarily choose to subscribe. Every sign-up will require consent and email confirmation, and subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.
